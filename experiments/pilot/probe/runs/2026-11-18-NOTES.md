## Direct or translated?

In all 7 answered problems the visible reasoning works **directly in Aleph**: no Python code, no Python-like lines, no mention of Python. Drafts in the reasoning are written in Aleph prefix syntax.

Most of the reasoning is about Aleph's rules rather than the algorithm: canonical order of functions, the `ex` requirement for helpers, names that may not shadow builtins, arity. Reasoning tokens per problem: 228 to 5,577.

## Limits

Seven problems, one model, one run each. Visible reasoning can show translation; it cannot prove there is none. Input tokens per call were about 13,600, most of them opencode's own system prompt, not `llms.txt`.
